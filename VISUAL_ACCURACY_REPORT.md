# Visual accuracy report

## Milestone 2 status

A native shell prototype now exists and can be captured in headless CI. This
is a structural implementation of the documented Home and Control Center
concepts, not a claim of pixel-accurate PS5 UI. Official textual references
confirm the features and control names but do not provide enough measurements
to validate exact geometry, typography, colors, or animation. Sony's
[PS5 Design story](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/design/stories/PS5/) was
not retrievable in the research environment (HTTP 403).

## Screenshots

## Reference and differences

References: Sony [PS5 features](https://www.playstation.com/en-th/ps5/ps5-features/),
[Control Center support](https://www.playstation.com/en-gb/support/games/customize-ps5-control-center/),
and [Welcome hub support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/account/welcome-hub/).
The [official 2024 Welcome hub screenshot](https://blog.playstation.com/tachyon/2024/09/2424168de190dae0ea094bf824184b7ec6c53e77.jpg?fit=1024%2C1024)
was inspected at 1024x576.

Implemented: the top-level Games/Media navigation and horizontal application
row follow the screenshot's hierarchy; a Welcome Hub-inspired local widget
area occupies the lower screen. Selecting a sample entry changes its artwork
palette and details. The Control Center is a separate focused overlay.
Keyboard, mouse, and mapped controller input operate the same focusable
components.

Observed differences from the reference: NEXUS uses short text labels rather
than Sony artwork/icons; its widgets are static, locally authored cards; the
background is an original vector wave pattern rather than selected media; it
does not reproduce the original screenshot's widget content, typography, or
exact card sizes. No Control Center reference image was obtained, so its
placement and transitions are not visually verified. No Sony artwork/fonts
were copied.

Guest launch and network/system services are unavailable and stated in the UI.
The current capture can be compared structurally but is not pixel-matched.

## Next validation

Capture a version- and region-specific public Sony UI reference, record its
resolution/context, measure component positions/colors, and compare it to
captures at the same aspect ratio. Update only after verifying the relevant
screen and behavior; do not distribute proprietary assets.
